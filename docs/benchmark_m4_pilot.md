# M4 pilot: flap cases against XFOIL

Four cases, NACA 2412 with a 25 % chord plain flap (`Airfoil.with_flap`, hinge 0.75 c, half
thickness), deflection 10 and 20 deg, Re 1e6 and 3e6, angles -4 to +20 deg in 1 deg steps, both codes
reading the same flapped coordinate file (160 panels), XFOIL 6.99 at 160 and 240 PANE nodes,
N_crit 9. Generated with `python -m foil_lab.benchmark.flap_run pilot --workers 4`; total 597 s
(XFOIL 8 jobs 10-77 s each; Foil-Lab 4 cases 153, 219, 251 and 503 s, 4 workers at once).

The comparison covers the points inside the attached-flow filter of SPEC.md 4.1 where both codes
converged. `ch` is Foil-Lab's sign (trailing edge down positive); XFOIL's `Chinge` is shown with the
sign changed. No target is applied to these numbers. The reference-reliability rule is applied to
Cl, Cd and Cm as in the main benchmark; `ch` has no rule yet.

Inviscid check on the same flapped files (NACA 2412, Re = 0, 0 and 4 deg): Cl differs from XFOIL's by
at most 0.003 for 0, 10, 20 and 30 deg deflection, Cm by at most 0.0005 (docs/xfoil_hinge_calibration.md
runs). The viscous differences below are therefore not a geometry or hinge-arc effect.
